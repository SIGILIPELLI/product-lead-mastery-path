# Product Lead Mastery Path

A structured, module-wise training program for **leading product teams** —
taking you from your first stretch assignment managing other PMs all the way
to CPO-level org design and strategy — with real frameworks, templates, and a
hands-on deliverable at the end of each level.

!!! info "This track is about leading product people, not being one"
    This site is for **Product Leads, Heads of Product, Group PMs, and
    aspiring CPOs** — the people-leadership and org-design track for those
    managing other product managers. If you're looking for the
    individual-contributor skill set instead (discovery, roadmapping,
    writing specs, shipping features as a single PM), see the companion
    [Product Manager Mastery Path](https://sigilipelli.github.io/product-manager-mastery-path/)
    — that track covers the IC job this one assumes you've already done.

## How the program is organized

| Level | Focus | Modules |
|-------|-------|---------|
| [Level 1 · Entry](level-1/index.md) | Making the jump from IC PM to leading a team | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 2 · Intermediate](level-2/index.md) | Leading multiple squads, hiring, coaching, managing up | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 3 · Advanced](level-3/index.md) | Head of Product: portfolios, org design, board communication | 9 topics + 1 project |
| [Level 4 · Master](level-4/index.md) | VP/CPO-level strategy, org-building, and legacy | 9 topics + 1 capstone |

## How to use this site

- Work through each level in order — later modules assume you've done the
  earlier ones and built on their deliverables.
- Every topic page gives you a real framework or template (as a table or
  worked structure, not code) plus a concrete worked example.
- Each topic ends with an **Exercise** — a practical deliverable you produce,
  not just a quiz.
- Each level ends with a project that combines everything learned in that
  level into one real artifact: a team charter, a hiring plan, an org design,
  or a full strategy document.
- Use the search bar (top of the page) to jump straight to a topic.

Start here → [Level 1 · Entry](level-1/index.md)

## What you'll need

No tools or subscriptions — just a willingness to draft real documents (team
charters, org charts, hiring plans, strategy memos) as you go. Every exercise
produces something you could genuinely hand to your own team or manager.
