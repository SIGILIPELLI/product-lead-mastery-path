# Product Lead Mastery Path

A free, structured, module-wise training program for leading product teams —
Product Lead / Head of Product / Group PM level — entry level to master
level, with real frameworks and templates in every module and a hands-on
deliverable at the end of each level.

**Live site:** https://sigilipelli.github.io/product-lead-mastery-path/

This track is about **leading** product teams and other PMs, not being an
individual-contributor PM. For the IC PM skill set (discovery, roadmapping,
specs, shipping), see the companion
[Product Manager Mastery Path](https://sigilipelli.github.io/product-manager-mastery-path/).

## Contents

- **Level 1 · Entry** — what a Product Lead is, leading a team, setting team
  vision, delegating decisions, cross-team alignment, mentoring PMs, running
  product rituals, basic org design, hiring PMs, and a capstone team charter
- **Level 2 · Intermediate** — leading multiple squads, performance
  management, cross-functional leadership, strategy cascading, advanced
  hiring, coaching, managing up, budget & resourcing
- **Level 3 · Advanced** — the Head of Product role, multi-product portfolio
  leadership, org design at scale, career ladders, executive communication,
  M&A integration, platform vs. product structures, board-level stakeholder
  management
- **Level 4 · Master** — VP/CPO-level strategy, building product orgs from
  scratch, turnarounds, board & investor relations, leadership pipelines,
  global org leadership, AI-native org design, and a capstone CPO strategy
  document
